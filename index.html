Smith Chart Matcher
  • Start at the load (device). Element 1 sits right at the device and the last element sits at the source. Drive the final point onto the orange target ring (Zs*).
  • Press + Add component (in the Matching network section) to add an element. Click an element's colored circle to bypass it, and hover a row for ↑ ↓ × (↑ moves it toward the device).
  • Series elements move along constant-r circles. Shunt elements move along constant-g circles (turn on the admittance grid to see them). The r = 1 and g = 1 circles are drawn darker.
  • Drag a component's dot on the chart to change its value. Series parts slide along their constant-r circle and shunt parts along their constant-g circle. The dot gently snaps onto a grid circle of constant g (series parts) or constant r (shunt parts) when it passes within a few pixels, and the snapped circle is shown dashed. Turn snapping off with snap while dragging in Setup, or hold Shift while dragging to skip it once. Zooming in gives finer snap circles.
  • Y circles (stubs): tick Y circles on a stub to draw, in its color, the constant-g circle it moves along (through its start point) and the constant-g circle through the target (g = 1 for a 50 Ω source). Other components snap onto them.
  • Z₀ circles: tick Z₀ circles on any series line in the Matching network to draw its constant-|Γ| circles (measured against that line's own Z₀), in the line's color. The dashed "through target" circle shows every point that line can rotate onto the target. The dotted "through start" circle is the full path the line can take from where it begins. Other components' dots snap onto them where their own path crosses.
  • View frequency (Setup): slide f (or press ▶ to sweep it back and forth) to see where every component's path, the input point and the readout land at that frequency. Component values don't change. Ideal lines keep θ defined at f₀, so their electrical length scales with f. Press = f₀ to return to the design frequency. The slider covers the sweep range in sweep mode, or ¼·f₀ to 2·f₀ otherwise.
  • Scroll on the chart to zoom (finer grid lines appear as you zoom in). Drag to pan, and double-click or press ⌂ to reset. Hover to read z and y.
  • Lines and stubs can be microstrip (W and L on the substrate, using the quasi-static Hammerstad–Jensen model, lossless) or ideal (Z₀ and θ at the design frequency).

▾Calculator

Numbers: 3+j4, 3+4j, j47.5, polar 50∠-30 (or 50@-30, degrees), 1e-15

Operators: + - * / ^ ( ) and a || b for parallel (binds tighter than + and −). Implicit multiply works: 2pi, j(3+4), jw

Powers: ^ takes the next number, name, function call or (group), including a leading minus. So 10^-15*5 is 10−15·5, not 10−75. The preview under the input draws exponents as superscripts and divisions as fractions so you can check the grouping before pressing Enter. Use parentheses for a longer exponent: 10^(-15*5).

SI suffixes: f p n u m k M G T, with an optional unit: 33.5fF, 100pH, 94GHz

Variables: zd = 16.5 - j47.5, then use zd. ans is the last result.

Constants: j pi e c, plus from the page: Z0 (chart Z₀), f0 (design f in Hz), w (2π·f0)

Functions: abs arg(°) re im conj inv sqrt exp ln log10 db sin cos tan deg rad polar(m,°) par(a,b,…)

RF helpers: zl(L,f) = jωL, zc(C,f) = 1/(jωC), Lx(X,f) = L for reactance X, Cx(X,f) = C for reactance X, gam(Z[,Z0]) = Γ, zg(Γ[,Z0]) = Z from Γ, vswr(Γ), zline(ZL,Zc,θ°) = input Z of a line

Editing: every row stays editable. Click any row and change it, and its result (plus every row below that uses ans or its variable) updates as you type.

Keys: Enter = add the new row (or move down) · ↑/↓ = move between rows · Backspace on an empty row deletes it · click a gray result to insert its value
